Data. Individual polls are those listed by RealClearPolling (race pages and the latest-polls listing, read Oct. 29, 2026), with Nebraska polls RCP does not carry taken from 270toWin. Each race’s average is this site’s own: the plain mean of the five most recent polls. Candidates and seat status come from 270toWin and Ballotpedia; race ratings are the Cook Political Report’s, as shown on 270toWin (Oct. 20 update). The New York Times keeps its own tracker, with a different poll set and averaging method, at nytimes.com.

Reading the numbers. A lead inside a poll’s margin of error is not a lead you can count on; a one-point average is a coin flip. Trend lines are a time-weighted smoothing of the listed polls, not anyone’s official average. This is a snapshot of public polling, not a forecast.
